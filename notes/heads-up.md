# Heads-Up No-Limit

# Preflop Strategy

## Facts

* BTN is also SB
* BTN/SB acts first preflop but last postflop

##  Strategy for 100bb effective stack

### BTN/SB open

* Open Size 2.25x
* Open Range
  * 84% of hands
  * fold J~3/2o, T~6/3o, 9~8/4o

### BB defense facing open

* Defending range is bigger size means tighter range to defend
* Defending range for 2.25x open is around 75%
* For 2.5x open 
  * 3Bet
    * value: 77+, AJo+, ATs+
    * bluff: suited gap connectors
  * Call
    * 66-
    * suited connectors
    * off-suited high cards
    * Ax
  * Fold

### BTN/SB defense facing 3 bet
  * Calls
    * low pocket pairs
    * strong and medium suited connectors
  * 4-Bet
    * bluff: off-suit high cards, weak Kxs, AQ/9/5o

     

## Side topics
### Bet size adjustment according to position and player type
* Bigger size as IP, since IP has more win rate, so win more
* Bigger size as OOP, because it could cut down the odds of IP player,if the IP could fold marginal hands, so it works with tight player and passive players, but not loose and aggresive ones
* This thinking also applies to 3bet, 4bet, 5bet
